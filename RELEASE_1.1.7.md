# Hagadol ERP 1.1.7 · CRM

Open https://wmkhagadol.hagadol-grou-7027.chatgpt.site/crm. The CRM has Pipeline, Contacts, Companies and Activities, following Bigin's core organisation of customer records and follow up work.

## Daily workflow

Add a customer company and link its contacts. Individuals can also be saved without a company. Add an opportunity, choose its owner, customer, value and stage, then record the next action, follow up date and expected closing date. Qualification details are under an expandable section.

Pipeline opens on active opportunities. Use the board or list view, search, owner and source filters, and Open, Closed or All views. Each board column shows its saved opportunity count and total value. Change stage using the card selector, which works with a keyboard. Export view exports the records matching the visible CRM filters.

Hagadol retains New, Qualified, Site assessment, Technical design, Quotation, Negotiation, Won and Lost. WMK retains New, Qualified, Design brief, Fee proposal, Negotiation, Won and Lost. Each has its own service lines, customer sectors and qualification fields. Group provides a combined view while individual records and their links stay within one business.

Open a contact, customer company or opportunity to review its details, related opportunities, linked contacts, activities and saved change history. Add a Task, Call or Meeting from the profile or pipeline card. Activities require an owner and deadline, can include a Kampala time, and can be logged as completed with notes. Activities supports Open, Due today, Overdue, Closed and All. Completion saves its date; reopening clears that date.

New opportunities and activities can link to saved contacts and customer companies. An activity linked to an opportunity inherits its customer references. Server validation blocks conflicting references and links across Hagadol and WMK. Duplicate email hints help review existing contacts. The contact importer retains its existing duplicate checks.

## Compatibility

Customer companies are a new record type inside the existing record payload storage. They are distinct from the Hagadol and WMK business selector. No database migration is needed. Existing contacts retain their organisation and person fields; existing free text customer names are not automatically merged or reclassified. Link those records explicitly when appropriate.

CRM activities use the shared Tasks records, with a CRM flag and activity type. They also appear in Tasks. EOS To dos keep their meeting links and remain outside CRM Activities. No accounting entries are generated by CRM saves.

Contact imports use the existing CSV importer. Automatic Bigin synchronisation, Gmail logging, calling, calendar invitations, campaign capture and external notifications are not activated by this release. Calls and meetings here are saved activity records. Bigin is the workflow benchmark, not a connected provider.

## Validation

The focused CRM checks cover customer references, industry stages, activity owners and dates, completion and reopening, stale edits, duplicate hints, unsafe websites, company separation, exports and form rendering. ERP API checks also exercise saved CRM references, duplicate request handling and version conflicts against an isolated database. Existing EOS checks verify the meeting flow remains compatible. TypeScript and the production build are checked before publication.

Browser click, keyboard and responsive visual acceptance remain pending because browser QA is unavailable in this environment. Server rendering checks do not prove interactive behaviour.
