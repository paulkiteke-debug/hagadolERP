WHAT TO TRY

CRM > Pipeline > Sales overview and targets:
Choose a period, inspect owner performance, click task counts to filter activities,
and set company or individual sales targets. Existing targets can be edited.
The Executive page also displays the sales overview for authorised users.

New deals need a positive UGX value and expected closing date. Historical missing
fields are flagged for review; no values or historical close dates are invented.

A drilling deal requires current verified hydrogeological survey evidence before
Technical design and later commercial stages. Open the deal > Deal evidence to
register a source document, submit it for review and have an Owner, Administrator
or Sales manager verify it. Return verified evidence to Draft to revoke it.
The verifier must review the actual source file. External file changes require a
new register revision; the ERP does not archive or hash the remote file content.

Before Won, retain all existing billing, technical, contract and signature checks.
Create a linked quote in Finance, then have an authorised reviewer approve it.
In Edit deal > More deal details, select the approved quote and enter the separate
Target project handover date. Quote total and contract value must match.

The Won transition creates one project in the retained project module, in Planning
and awaiting assignment. It does not issue an invoice, record cash or post revenue.
Projects remain deferred in the default CRM and Finance release. The CRM shows
that the project was created and its target delivery date.

When the project module is activated later, completing its existing final evidence
gate creates one internal CRM service review reminder due 30 days later. This is
an internal planning date to confirm with the customer. No messages are sent.
Customer testimonial evidence may be registered and reviewed. Verification does
not grant publication consent or automatically label a customer an advocate.

For a local clickable demonstration, open:
review/Hagadol_CRM_Design_Preview.html
It uses synthetic data and no login or database. User permissions and remote
connections must be accepted separately in the authenticated Preview.

No live database, production deployment, staff account or external customer
communication was changed. Production rollout remains a separate step.

BILLING REVIEW
Open CRM > Companies > select the customer company. Save its billing address and
Tax PIN or TIN. Owner, Administrator or Finance can then select Verify billing
details after reviewing those values. A Sales manager's quote approval does not
replace this separate billing review.
